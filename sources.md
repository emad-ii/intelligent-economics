# Sources

This working tree cites three official objects. It does not replace them.

## 1. Paper — *Intelligent Economics*

Emad Mostaque, May 2026, 40 pages. *A foundation for the last economy.*

Public PDFs:

- https://webstatics.ii.inc/microsites/common-wealth/pdfs/intelligent-economics.pdf
- https://thesis.ii.inc/papers/intelligent-economics.pdf

A copy of the public PDF is at `papers/intelligent-economics.pdf` (fetched from the webstatics URL).

The paper derives. Interactive pages in this tree re-derive recoveries the paper already states. They do not invent extra theory.

## 2. Site

- https://ie.ii.inc

The official argument site. This repository is a working tree of interactive derivations, not a second official site.

## 3. Book — *The Last Economy*

- https://thelasteconomy.com

The book names (Intelligence Theory, Sorter's Law, the Intelligence Lagrangian, and the rest). This tree does not copy book text. Link only.

## What is not included

- Book text, quotations, or a TLE rewrite
- Common Wealth material
- A textbook treatment of classical fields
- Pages for notes-only or absent fields
- Pages for tax/ETI, Solow, cycles, public goods, IV, unemployment, open economy, CAPM, term structure, development, gravity, crypto, or fiscal theory

Residual human value stays open, as the paper leaves it.
