Akerlof identified

Asymmetric information is a support dynamics. The buyer prices average quality under the current reference, sellers above the price exit, and the reference updates to the survivors: $\mu\leftarrow(\mathrm{selection}\circ\mathrm{tilt})$. That is the Lucas box with a support-restriction step.

The paper identifies the market for quality with the across-round map already forced as $\mu\leftarrow\rho^*$ (section 6.1; Fig. 6). Akerlof adds one operation: selection tightens the support. The same iteration, without that step, is expectations adjustment; run on a system's own output with finite sampling, it is model collapse. One map wearing three literatures.

$$\mu\leftarrow(\mathrm{selection}\circ\mathrm{tilt}),\qquad \rho^*\propto\mu\,e^{V/\tau}$$

What the paper states

From section 6.1: asymmetric information enters the same frame as a support dynamics. In the market for quality [Akerlof, 1970], the buyer prices average quality under the current reference, sellers above the price exit, and the reference updates to the survivors: $\mu\leftarrow(\mathrm{selection}\circ\mathrm{tilt})$, the across-round iteration of the Lucas box with a support-restriction step, so the unravelling cascade of adverse selection is the same annealing that carries expectations to their rational limit and generative models to collapse: one map wearing three literatures.

The repair is the openness law applied to markets: warranties and disclosure make quality contractible and thereby pin the support open by commitment, holding the region the iteration would otherwise shed; in the Akerlof benchmark, where gains from trade are positive at every quality, every type then trades. That repair is not simulated here. Signalling admits the temperature-indexed reading, pooling at high $\tau$ and separation as $\tau$ falls [Spence, 1973]; that reading is not simulated here.

From section 5.1 and Fig. 6 on ie.ii.inc: the update $\mu\leftarrow\rho^*$ with $\rho^*\propto\mu\,e^{V/\tau}$ is forced; reading Lucas, Akerlof, and model collapse as instances of it is identified. Iterated under a fixed objective the update anneals toward a point mass: long-run rational expectations, and the doxic monoculture whose fragility section 8 prices. The same across-round iteration, run by a system on its own output, is the noiseless skeleton of model collapse [Shumailov et al., 2024]: finite sampling loses the tails the next reference can no longer restore.
